Aloo Paratha is a versatile North Indian delicacy eaten in breakfast, lunch and dinner all the same

Above that, its super easy to make!

    Ingredients for a serving of 3
  • 4 - 5 medium-sized potatoes
  • 2 cup wheat flour
  • 1/2 cup water
  • pinch of carrom seeds
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 medium sized onion (don't be shy!)
  • green chillies
  • ginger
  • jeera
  • spices
  • And loads of Ghee!
      • Cooking Process:
  • Wash your potatoes thoroughly in ice-cold water.
  • Cut them into chunky-square pieces and boil them until soft with a pinch of salt.
  • While the potatoes are being boiled, knead the dough with another pinch of salt and ajwain.
  • If you want crunchy parathas use a bit of ghee or oil while kneading otherwise just use water!
  • Let the dough rest for 10 minutes.
  • Mash the potatoes with spices, onions, greenchillies, ginger and some jeera!
  • Take a handful of dough and roll them into circles.
  • Take another handful (a bit smaller than the last handful) of the potato-masala and shape into a ball.
  • Place the potato ball in middle of the rolled dough and wrap the potato ball around with dough.
  • Roll it out into a circle again.
  • Apply some ghee on your tawa and fry the rolled dough until it cooks properly from both sides
  • DO NOT forget to flip it over once cooked from the bottom.
  • Enjoy your hot parathas with some curd and roast cumin(jeera) powder or a bit more of ghee!
